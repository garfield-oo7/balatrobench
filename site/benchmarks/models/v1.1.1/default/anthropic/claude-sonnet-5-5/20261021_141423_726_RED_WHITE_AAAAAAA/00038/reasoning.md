Flush is our main hand. Hand has Clubs K, Q, T, 3 (4 clubs), so converting to Clubs would be better, but The World only makes Spades. Spades A and 9 are already there. Converting three cards to Spades gives a 5-card spade flush: A, 9 plus K of H, Q of D and one more. I picked K of H, Q of D and 9 of S, which gives A, K, Q, 9 as spades. That's only 4 spades, and it needs one more. Clubs are still the better route, but the World is still the best option in this pack.
