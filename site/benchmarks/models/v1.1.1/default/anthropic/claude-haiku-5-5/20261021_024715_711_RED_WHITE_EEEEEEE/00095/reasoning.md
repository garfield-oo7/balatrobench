With the Smeared Joker, all five red cards (D_A, H_J, D_5, H_2, D_2) count as a flush since red suits merge into one. That gives flush level 5 scoring: 95 chips and 12 mult, plus card chips totaling 30 (11+10+5+2+2).

Adding joker effects: Even Steven gives +4 mult per even card, so the two 2s add +8; Scholar adds +20 chips and +4 mult for the Ace; Supernova adds mult equal to times Flush has been played, roughly 21. That brings total chips to about 145 and mult to about 45, for a score near 6525 — enough to clear the remaining 6315 needed against the blind. I'll play all five red cards.
