Looking at my hand of 7 cards, I count five hearts among them (Q, J, T, 9, 7) — that's a flush right there.

Playing all five gives base chips from card values plus bonuses: Scary Face adds for face cards, Crafty adds extra, and Odd Todd boosts odd-ranked cards like 9 and 7. Adding it all up with the flush base and mult of 4, I land around 1132 score against a target of 600 — well worth playing this hand.
