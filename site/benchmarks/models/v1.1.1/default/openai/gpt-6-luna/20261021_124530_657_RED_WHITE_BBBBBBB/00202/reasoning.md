Play the available Level 5 Three of a Kind using all three 3s. Mystic Summit and Fortune Teller add +31 Mult to the hand's base 11 Mult, and Ramen doubles the resulting Mult for a strong score of roughly 10,000 chips. Photograph will not trigger because the Jack is not one of the scored triplet cards, so use the two extra slots to cycle the Jack and other non-scoring cards while keeping the scoring combination intact.
