Play a Full House using three Sixes and both Twos, leaving the fourth Six, Eight, and Jack held. The Arm reduces the hand level by one, but it is already Level 1, so it stays at the 40-chip/4-mult base. Zany Joker triggers for the three-of-a-kind component (+12 Mult), and Raised Fist sees the held Six as the lowest rank (+12 Mult). That gives roughly (40 + 18 + 4) × (4 + 12 + 12) = 1,736, enough to clear the 1,472 points remaining.
