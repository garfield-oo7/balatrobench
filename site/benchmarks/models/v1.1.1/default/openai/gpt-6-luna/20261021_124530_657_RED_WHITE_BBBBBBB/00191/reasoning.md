Play a five-card High Card using the Queen as the scored card while cycling four other cards. The selected ranks are all distinct, so this avoids turning the hand into a Pair; the Queen's Red Seal retriggers its scoring, and Photograph doubles the first face-card Mult. Mystic Summit (+15 Mult) and Ramen (×2 Mult) make this a much stronger opening score than a basic Pair. Preserve the two 9s for a future Two Pair if we draw another matching rank.
