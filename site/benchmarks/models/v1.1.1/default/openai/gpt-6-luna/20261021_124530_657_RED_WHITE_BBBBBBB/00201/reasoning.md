Play the Level 9 Two Pair using both Aces and both Queens; it is the strongest available hand by far. Fortune Teller and Mystic Summit add +31 Mult, while the scored Queen triggers Photograph and Ramen supplies another ×2 multiplier. The red-seal Queen also retriggers, so include it among the scoring cards. Use the King as the fifth, unscored card to cycle one more card without breaking Two Pair.
