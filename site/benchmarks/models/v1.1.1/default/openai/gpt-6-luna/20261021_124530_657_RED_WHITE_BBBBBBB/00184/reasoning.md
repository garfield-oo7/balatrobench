Play the two pair formed by the debuffed Kings and the scoring 9s. The Pillar's debuffs do not erase the Kings from hand recognition, so this activates the Level 9 Two Pair base (180 chips/10 Mult) and Mad Joker's +10 Mult. The 9 of Clubs also scores its +4 Mult enhancement; Mystic Summit adds +15 and Ramen then doubles the Mult. Include the unscored 8 as a fifth card to cycle it since discards are unavailable.
