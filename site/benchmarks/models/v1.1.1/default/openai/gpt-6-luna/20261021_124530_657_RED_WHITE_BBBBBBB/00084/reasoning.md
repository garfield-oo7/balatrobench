Play the five lowest cards (9, 7, 5, 4, 2), leaving Q-J-T held. This scores a High Card 9 while Raised Fist sees the lowest held card, the Ten, for +20 Mult; Mystic Summit adds +15 Mult because discards are at zero. The resulting score is much stronger than playing the Queen while leaving the low 2 held, and cycles five cards toward a pair or a hand that triggers the other Jokers.
