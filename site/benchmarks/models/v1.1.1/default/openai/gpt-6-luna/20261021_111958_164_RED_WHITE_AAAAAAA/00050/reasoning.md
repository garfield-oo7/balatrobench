Take The Sun and convert the Ace of Spades, King of Clubs, and 9 of Spades to Hearts, completing a five-card Heart flush with the existing 7♥ and 4♥. This gives an immediate strong flush option (about 600 chips here) when the shown hand has no pair or trips. Sacrificing the King’s Club suit costs one Ancient Joker trigger, but keeps the other Clubs intact; Judgement is unusable with all Joker slots occupied.
