Play the pair of sixes with the Two of Hearts as an unscored third card. The two scored even sixes trigger Even Steven for +8 Mult, and the three-card play preserves Half Joker's +20 Mult, for approximately (10 base chips + 12 rank chips) × (2 + 8 + 20) = 660 points. That should bring the blind close enough to finish with one of the two remaining hands; cycling the low Two is more useful than spending a discard, since none remain.
