Play the five Spades as a Flush. The Level-1 Flush contributes 35 base chips and 4 base Mult; the card ranks add 32 chips, and Swashbuckler adds +11 Mult, for roughly 67 × 15 = 1,005 points (possibly more from scoring effects). This is substantially stronger than a three-card High Card with Half Joker, and there are no Hearts to trigger Ancient Joker. With no discards available, cycling all five cards also gives the best chance to find a strong follow-up hand.
