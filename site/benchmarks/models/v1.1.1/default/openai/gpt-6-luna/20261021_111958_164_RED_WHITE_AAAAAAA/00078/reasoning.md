Play the Tens and Sevens as a Level-2 Two Pair for the strongest reliable score: its 40 base chips plus 34 rank chips are multiplied by the base 3 Mult and Swashbuckler's +11, for about 1,036 points. Include the Eight as an unscored fifth card to cycle the maximum number of cards, while preserving the pair of Threes and Six for follow-up hands. Half Joker does not trigger on a four-card Two Pair, but this line beats the three-card pair option.
