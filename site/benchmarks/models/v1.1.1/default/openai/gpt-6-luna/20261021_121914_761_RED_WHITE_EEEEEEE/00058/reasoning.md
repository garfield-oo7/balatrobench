Play the Kings and Jacks as Two Pair, the strongest scoring hand available and the hand type with substantial Level 4 scaling plus Jolly Joker and Supernova bonuses. The fifth card, the Three, is unscored and cycles out without breaking the pair of Sevens or the Ten, preserving those for the final hand. This is preferable to playing the Sevens now because the Kings and Jacks provide greater card value.
