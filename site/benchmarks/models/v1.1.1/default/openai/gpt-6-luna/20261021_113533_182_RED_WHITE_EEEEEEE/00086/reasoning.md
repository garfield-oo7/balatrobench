Play a Level 3 Full House using three 5s (including the Glass 5) and the pair of 3s. This scores the Glass multiplier while triggering Jolly Joker, the +3 Supernova Full House bonus, and Ride the Bus without scoring a face card. The Full House base (90 chips, 8 mult) plus card values and these joker bonuses should exceed the 5,000-point Needle target in this single allowed hand; playing all four 5s instead would make Four of a Kind and score substantially less.
