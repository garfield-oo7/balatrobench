Play the pair of Aces with both visible even-rank cards and one face-down card to cycle five cards while keeping a chance of improving the hand to Two Pair or better. Two Pair is our strongest reliable engine: at Level 3 it has 60 chips/4 Mult, and Jolly Joker plus Supernova add substantial Mult; any scored even card also triggers Even Steven. The House only hides the initial view, so playing the unknown card can still reveal and score a stronger combination.
