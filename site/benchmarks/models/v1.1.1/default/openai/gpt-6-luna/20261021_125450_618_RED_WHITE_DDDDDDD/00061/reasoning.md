Play the five-card Diamond Flush. The Club debuffs only Clubs, so this hand is safe and activates both Flush Jokers (+80 Chips and +10 Mult); the two Mult-enhanced scoring cards add +8 Mult, while Abstract adds +15 Mult. Using the higher-rank 6 and 5 rather than the 3 maximizes scored card chips, and Hiker permanently improves all five scored cards. This should comfortably clear the 4,000 target in one hand.
