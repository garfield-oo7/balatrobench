Play the two pair of Aces and Fours, using the fifth card to cycle another card. This gives a much stronger immediate score than risking a one-card draw for a fifth Club: Abstract adds +15 Mult, the two scored Hearts add +6 Mult and Lusty’s Foil adds +50 chips, while Ancient may multiply the two scored cards of its active suit. It also preserves two hands to finish the remaining target.
