The remaining hand cannot make a Flush (only four Spades) or a stronger combination, so use the Bonus Ace of Spades as High Card. It scores its rank and +30 Bonus chips, while Blue Joker and Abstract Joker still add chips/mult; keeping the Steel King unplayed preserves its ×1.5 held-in-hand Mult bonus. The Ace may also receive Ancient Joker's ×1.5 if Spades are its current suit. Playing the pair of threes would add Lusty's Heart Mult but has substantially fewer chips and is not a better expected score.
