Choose Deja Vu and add a Red Seal to the Ten of Spades. A Red Seal retriggers the card when scored, amplifying its chips and any relevant suit-based Joker effects; this is a durable upgrade for a card that can be included in a Spade Flush, and it may trigger Ancient Joker twice when Spades are its active suit. This is more consistently valuable for the Flush plan than a Gold Seal’s cash or the highly disruptive Wraith with all five Joker slots occupied.
