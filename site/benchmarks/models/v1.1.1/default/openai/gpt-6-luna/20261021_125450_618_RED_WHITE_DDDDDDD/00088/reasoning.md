Play the five Diamonds as a Level 3 Flush to activate Crafty (+80 Chips) and Droll (+10 Mult). The Mult-enhanced D_Q adds further Mult, while Abstract and Blue Joker provide strong flat bonuses; this should score about 10,000, enough with the existing 2,288 to clear the 11,000 blind immediately. Save the remaining hands for the round payout rather than spending an extra hand to activate Acrobat.
