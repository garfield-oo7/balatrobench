Play the Pair of Tens while using the Jack, 7, and 3 as cycling cards. This scores the strongest available hand and keeps the remaining three Spades (9, 5, 2) intact as a base for a possible Flush on the next draw, where Crafty and Droll provide the major scoring bonuses. The Tens also trigger Hiker when scored; playing five cards maximizes the redraw without sacrificing those retained Spades.
