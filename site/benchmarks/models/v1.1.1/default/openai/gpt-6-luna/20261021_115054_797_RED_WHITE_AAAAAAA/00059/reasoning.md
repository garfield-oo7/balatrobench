Play the Aces and pair of 3s as Level 4 Two Pair. This activates Mad Joker’s +10 Mult; Half Joker does not activate on four cards. The remaining 5 is the lowest held card, so Raised Fist adds +10 Mult. The hand scores about (80 base chips + 28 card chips) × (5 base Mult + 10 Mad Joker + 10 Raised Fist) = 2,700, enough to cover the 2,660 points still needed and finish the blind in one hand.
