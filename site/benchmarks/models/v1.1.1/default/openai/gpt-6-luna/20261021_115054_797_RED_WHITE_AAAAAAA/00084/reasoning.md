Play the guaranteed pair of Fours together with both face-down cards and the low-value Nine. This cycles five cards with no discards, giving the hidden cards a chance to improve the hand to Two Pair or better and trigger Mad Joker alongside the level-6 Two Pair score; if it remains only a Pair, To Do List can still pay. Preserve the Ace and Queen, and keep the Gold 3 in hand for its end-of-round payout.
