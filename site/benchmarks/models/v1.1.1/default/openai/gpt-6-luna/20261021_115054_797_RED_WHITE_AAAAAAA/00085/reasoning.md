Play the Queens and the Club/Diamond 3s for a Level 6 Two Pair, activating Mad Joker’s +10 Mult and Swashbuckler’s +10 Mult. This scores far more than the 800 points remaining (about 3,900 before any Steel effect), so it should clear the blind immediately. Keep the Steel 3 in hand to retain its held-in-hand ×1.5 Mult effect during scoring, and keep the Ace, Ten, Seven, and Steel card rather than needlessly cycling cards when the blind is already beatable.
