Play the Queens and sixes as a Level 2 Two Pair. This activates Mad Joker (+10 Mult), and both scored sixes trigger Even Steven (+8 Mult total); Holographic Runner adds +10 Mult. The scoring cards contribute about 32 chips, so the hand should score roughly (40 base + 32 card chips) × (3 base + 10 + 8 + 10) = 2,232, enough to exceed the remaining 2,040 points and clear the blind in one hand. Playing only these four cards also preserves Half Joker’s +20 Mult.
