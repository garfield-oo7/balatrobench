The Mouth has already been locked to Pair by the earlier hands, so Two Pair is not a legal scoring option despite the two pairs available. Play the pair of Fives, which is the highest-value Pair available, and keep the Steel Three in hand for its held-in-hand ×1.5 Mult effect and the Gold Three for its end-of-round payout. Hanging Chad retriggers the first scoring Five twice; playing only the pair preserves those held-card benefits on this final hand.
