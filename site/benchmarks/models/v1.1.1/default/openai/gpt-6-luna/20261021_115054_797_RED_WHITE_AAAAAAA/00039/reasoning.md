Choose The Sun and convert the 8♣, 6♣, and 2♣ to Hearts. Alongside the 7♥ and 4♥ already in hand, this creates a five-card Heart flush option while making all three converted cards trigger Even Steven when scored. This adds useful flush flexibility to the existing Two Pair engine; Judgement cannot create a Joker with all five slots occupied, and the other suit conversions do not combine as well with the suits already present.
