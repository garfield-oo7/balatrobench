Play Two Pair with the Queens and Fours, adding the off-suit Club 6 as a non-scoring fifth card to cycle the maximum number of cards. This preserves all four Diamonds (9, 8, 5, 4) for a possible Flush, avoids spending a discard and keeps Delayed Gratification eligible for its payout. Wrathful Joker and The Idol do not trigger on this hand; The Arm may reduce its hand level, but Two Pair is still the best scoring hand available.
