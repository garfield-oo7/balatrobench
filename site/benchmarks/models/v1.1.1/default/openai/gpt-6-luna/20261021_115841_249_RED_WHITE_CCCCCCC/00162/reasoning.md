Play the Level-6 Flush using the four Clubs plus the Wild 9 of Diamonds, which counts as a Club for the flush. This is the strongest available scoring hand: it benefits from Flush's 110 chips/14 mult, Supernova's +23 mult, Bootstraps' +12 mult, and Hiker/Odd Todd chip bonuses on scored cards. The hand cycles five cards while retaining the Ace of Spades and Jack of Hearts for the next draw.
