Take The Lovers and make the 9 of Diamonds (hand index 5) Wild. This preserves its rank while letting it count toward whichever suit we need for the Level 4 Flush engine; as a Wild 9 it can also count as a Heart for The Idol’s current 9-of-Hearts trigger. That flexible, permanent deck upgrade is stronger than shifting a few cards into one fixed suit, and Judgement cannot create a Joker while all five slots are occupied.
