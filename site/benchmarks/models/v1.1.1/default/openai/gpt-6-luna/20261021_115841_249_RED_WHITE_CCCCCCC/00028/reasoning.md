Play Two Pair using the Aces and Eights, with the Spade Ace and Spade Eight both scoring for Wrathful Joker. Red Card adds +3 Mult, and the two scored Spades add another +6, so this is substantially stronger than the other available two-pair combinations. Include the Spade Two as a fifth cycling card; it is only a kicker and won’t score, but this maximizes the draw while preserving all four discards for Delayed Gratification.
