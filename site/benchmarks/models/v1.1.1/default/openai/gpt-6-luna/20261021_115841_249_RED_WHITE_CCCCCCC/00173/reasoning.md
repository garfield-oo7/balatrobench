Play the five Spades as the Level-6 Flush, preserving the off-suit Queen. This is far stronger than the available Two Pair and triggers the flush bonuses from Supernova (+24 Mult), Bootstraps (+16 Mult), and Mystic Summit (+15 Mult, since discards are at zero). Both A♠ cards score for The Idol, and the enhanced A♠ adds Mult; scoring all five also permanently builds Hiker's chip bonuses.
