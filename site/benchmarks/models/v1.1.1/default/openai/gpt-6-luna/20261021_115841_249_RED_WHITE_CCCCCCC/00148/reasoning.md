Skip the Spectral pack. Ankh is an extremely risky reset with five occupied Joker slots: it would retain only a random duplicated Joker and destroy the other four, with no reliable way to preserve the current Flush scoring engine. Familiar adds three cards and destroys one at random, which dilutes the established Flush deck without a compelling payoff. The existing Level-6 Flush lineup is already cohesive, so avoiding these unnecessary risks is best.
