Play the pair of Kings with the Ten, 4, and 2 as Splash-scored cards. This activates Jolly Joker and the Pair-based Supernova, scores the Mult-enhanced King, and gives three Even Steven triggers from the Ten, 4, and 2. Although it gives up the Steel King’s held-in-hand bonus for this hand, this is the strongest available scoring hand and cycles five cards with only two hands left.
