Play the Aces and Jacks as Two Pair, the strongest available hand at Level 5 and the one that triggers Mad Joker. Its base 100 Chips and 6 Mult are boosted by Mad Joker (+10), Runner's Holographic edition (+10), and Swashbuckler (+13), for roughly 5,500 points before any other effects. Include the 7 of Clubs as an unscored fifth card to cycle it, while keeping the 7 of Spades in hand so the pair remains available for a later Two Pair.
