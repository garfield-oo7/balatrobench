Play the known pair of Eights together with the Club 9 and both face-down cards. This guarantees at least a Pair, while the hidden cards can upgrade it to Two Pair or better and activate Mad Joker’s +10 Mult; including the Club 9 also preserves a chance for Ancient Joker’s suit-based multiplier if it scores.
