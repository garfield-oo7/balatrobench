Keep both spades and the pair of fives; draw four toward spade-backed pairs, two pair, or a full house. Wrathful Joker makes scoring spades more valuable than pursuing the four-diamond flush, which would score only about 480. Two pair with kings and sevens would score (20 + 20 + 14 + 50) × (2 + 6) = 832, enough to win immediately. Trading Card has already activated, so a multi-card discard loses no income.
