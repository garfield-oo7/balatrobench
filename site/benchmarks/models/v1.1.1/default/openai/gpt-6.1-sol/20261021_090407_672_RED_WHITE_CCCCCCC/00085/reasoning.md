Use The World to convert the Ace, King, and Ten of Clubs into Spades. This permanently adds three high-value spades, improving access to our level-3 Flush and giving each converted card +3 Mult when scored through Wrathful Joker. It also creates five spades in this pack hand and a second Ace of Spades for future matching-rank hands. Better flush consistency and repeated suit bonuses are worth more than Temperance's unneeded $10 or skipping for Red Card's +3 Mult.
