Turn the Ace of Spades into Glass, then play it first in an A-K-Q-Q-J Flush. Bootstraps is disabled, but Hanging Chad is active and will score the Glass Ace three times, giving ×8 to the Flush's base 16 Mult. With 198 chips, Red Card +45 Mult, Cavendish ×3 and Ramen ×2, this scores 198 × (16×8+45) ×6 = 205,524, comfortably exceeding the 100,000 target in one hand. No discards or Steel enhancement are needed.
