Use The World to convert the Heart Queen, Heart Eight, and Club Six into Spades. This converts all three off-suit cards in the offered hand, permanently improving consistency of our level-7 Flush engine and giving those cards Wrathful Joker's +3 Mult when scored. Reliable three-card suit conversion is more valuable than Red Card's modest +3 Mult from skipping; none of the other choices improves our established spade strategy as effectively.
