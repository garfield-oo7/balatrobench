Cycle the three non-club, non-Steel cards as a 9-high hand, preserving all four clubs and the Steel 3. This scores approximately 161 chips while drawing three chances to find the fifth club. A level-3 club flush with five Ancient Joker triggers will comfortably cover the remaining target, and four hands will remain if these draws miss. Do not play the pair of Twos: that would consume a valuable club and weaken our flush setup.
