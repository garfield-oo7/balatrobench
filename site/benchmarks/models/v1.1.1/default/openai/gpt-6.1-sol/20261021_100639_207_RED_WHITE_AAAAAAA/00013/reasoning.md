Buy holographic Runner primarily for its unconditional +10 Mult, not its straight condition. It fixes our dependence on finding two pair with no discards: a single scored face card now gives (5+10+30) × (1+10) = 495, and a face-card pair gives (10+20+60) × (2+10) = 1,080.
