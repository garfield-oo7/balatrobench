Keep both Mult-enhanced 4s and the Queen; discard five unmatched cards to seek level-8 Three of a Kind or Two Pair while preserving all four hands. Three 4s played alone activate Half Joker and should comfortably beat 20,000 with their enhancements, Abstract, Bull, and Ramen. A second pair is a strong fallback. Removing the 2 also improves Raised Fist, and the Queen offers a high-rank pairing target. The small Ramen reduction from ×1.96 to ×1.91 is worthwhile for finding a strong upgraded hand rather than spending a hand on a weak pair.
