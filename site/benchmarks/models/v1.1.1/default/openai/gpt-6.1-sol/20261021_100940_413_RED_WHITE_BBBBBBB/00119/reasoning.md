Play Jack high with Eight, Five, Four, and Three as cycling cards, holding Ace, Ten, and the Blue-Seal Nine. Raised Fist then supplies +18 Mult and all four discards preserve Banner's +120 Chips: (5 + 10 + 120) × (1 + 18 + 15) = 4,590 chips. This clears the low cards and draws five replacements toward level-6 trips, while retaining the Blue Seal for a potential planet at round end. Discarding the Purple Seal could generate a Tarot, but is unnecessary here and would reduce Banner's scoring on every subsequent hand.
