Choose Venus to raise our primary Three of a Kind to level 7: 150 base Chips and 15 Mult. Trips activates Zany's +12 Mult, and Ramen doubles the added Mult after our other bonuses. This improves our strongest established scoring hand while preserving the no-discard strategy supporting Banner and Ramen. Uranus is a useful fallback upgrade, but Venus provides +20 Chips and +2 Mult rather than Uranus's +20 Chips and +1 Mult.
