Play A♠, 10♣, 8♠, 6♠, and 4♠ as a black Flush through Smeared Joker. Four even ranks add +16 Mult, and this tenth Flush gives +10 from Supernova. After The Flint halves the base to 40 Chips and 5 Mult, Hologram yields (40+11+10+8+6+4) × (5+16+10) × 1.25 = approximately 3,061 points. This leaves only about 939 points with three hands and two discards remaining. Take this strong guaranteed score now rather than spending discards chasing an unenhanced flush that cannot reach 4,000 in one hand.
