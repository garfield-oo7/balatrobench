Set up a level-7 Spade Flush with both Aces, Ten, Lucky King, and one ordinary King, retaining the other King and Steel 6. Score all three non-face Spades before the first King so Photograph doubles their Wrathful Mult as well; place the Lucky King first among faces so a Lucky +20 Mult proc is also doubled. Avoid playing both Aces with all three Kings, which would become the weaker level-1 Flush House. This flush should score roughly 35,000 before any permanent chip bonuses or Lucky proc, making two hands a realistic clear while preserving Justice.
