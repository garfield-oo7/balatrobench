Prepare a level-7 Spade Flush with Ace, Ten, Nine, Lucky King, and one ordinary King. Put all three non-face Spades before the Lucky King so Photograph doubles their accumulated Wrathful Mult and any Lucky +20 Mult proc. This should score about 25,000 without a Lucky proc, putting the 70,000 target within three strong flushes. Retain the extra King for the next hand and save Justice and The World for later draws or an emergency.
