Take Ouija: all eight offered hand cards are Spades, so converting them to one rank creates eight identical-rank Spades and preserves our suit synergy. This unlocks repeatable Flush Five (160 base Chips, 16 Mult), a major upgrade over our level-3 Flush (65 Chips, 8 Mult), while retaining Wrathful bonuses and existing card modifiers. Four/Five of a Kind and Flush House also become stronger fallback options. The permanent -1 hand size is a real cost, but concentrating eight suited cards into one rank is worth it, especially with Trading Card continuing to thin off-suit cards. Wraith is unsuitable with all Joker slots filled and would erase our $64 economy and Bootstraps bonus.
