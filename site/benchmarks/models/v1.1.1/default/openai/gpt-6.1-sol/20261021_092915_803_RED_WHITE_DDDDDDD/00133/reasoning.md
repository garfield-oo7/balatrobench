Keep A♠, Q♠, 5♠, and 2♠; discard both Clubs and both red cards to draw four chances at the fifth Spade. A Spade flush benefits strongly from Wrathful, Fibonacci, and Even Steven, and can exceed 20,000 with Blackboard active. We still have two discards afterward to complete the flush or clear newly drawn red cards from the held hand. Preserve The Star rather than spend it on an incomplete Diamond conversion.
