Choose The Emperor with both consumable slots empty to generate two Tarot cards. These can provide targeted Spade conversions, Glass/Steel enhancements, deck thinning, or economy; unwanted Tarots can instead be sold to increase Campfire from X3.75 to as much as X4.25 at no additional purchase cost. Strength offers little for this hand and cannot improve our Spade Ace, while a Wild card is vulnerable to every suit-debuff boss and offers less reliable value than two stored Tarots.
