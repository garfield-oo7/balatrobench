Play J♥ with 10♦, 7♦, and 3♥ as non-scoring extras, drawing four cards while keeping all four spades (10♠, 9♠, 5♠, 2♠). The J♥ scores about 67 chips through Scary Face and Ancient Joker. Any fifth spade then completes a flush worth at least (35 + 10 + 9 + 5 + 2 + 2) × (4 + 15 + 10) = 1,827, enough to clear the remaining blind. Keeping four spades is more reliable than breaking the flush draw for a small pair score; two hands remain afterward if another cycling play is needed.
