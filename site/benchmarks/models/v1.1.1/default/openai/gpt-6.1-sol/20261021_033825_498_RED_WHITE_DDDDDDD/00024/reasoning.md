Play the queen pair with A♦, 8♣, and 2♥ as unscored cycling cards, retaining 10♠, 9♠, and 5♠. The queens score about 270 chips through Scary Face and Ancient Joker's heart multiplier, while drawing five fresh cards without discards. Keeping three spades gives the strongest route to a winning flush: Wrathful plus Droll provides 29 Mult, enough to clear the remaining target even without face cards. Hiker also permanently upgrades both queens.
