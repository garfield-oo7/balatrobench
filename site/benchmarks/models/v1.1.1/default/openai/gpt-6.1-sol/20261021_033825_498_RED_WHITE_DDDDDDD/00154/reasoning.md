Discard the ordinary Club Queen and Diamond 5 to seek the fifth Spade, retaining all four Spades and both held Steel cards. A Spade flush with the red-seal 6 supplies six Ancient Joker and Arrowhead triggers, while the two Steel cards multiply the resulting Mult by 2.25.
