Apply Deja Vu's Red Seal to the Spade 6. Each scoring retrigger adds its card chips, +50 Chips from Arrowhead, +3 Mult from Wrathful Joker, and +4 Mult from Even Steven; when Ancient Joker targets Spades, it also supplies another ×1.5 Mult trigger. This is the strongest immediate scoring upgrade available and remains useful after Ancient changes suit. Prefer it to duplicating unenhanced cards or sacrificing all our money for Wraith with no open Joker slot.
