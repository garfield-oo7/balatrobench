Play J♥ with 10♦, 7♦, and 3♥ as cycling cards, preserving all four spades (10♠, 9♠, 5♠, 2♠). With no discards, drawing four cards gives our best chance to complete a winning spade flush while retaining two hands afterward. Any fifth spade is sufficient: even a low flush scores at least (35 + 10 + 9 + 5 + 2 + 2) × (4 + 15 + 10) = 1,827, exceeding the remaining 1,330. The heart jack also earns Scary Face chips, Ancient's ×1.5, and a permanent Hiker upgrade.
