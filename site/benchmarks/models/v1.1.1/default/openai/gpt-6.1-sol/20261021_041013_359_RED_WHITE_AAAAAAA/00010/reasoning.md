Play J-7-6-5 as High Card for 45 chips (5 base + 10 Jack + 30 Scary Face), while retaining the pair of 8s and King. With no discards, this cycles four cards toward Two Pair or Full House. Any second pair alongside the 8s activates Mad Joker and scores at least 480 chips; a pair of 5s or higher clears the remaining 555, and paired Kings score far more thanks to Scary Face. Keeping the existing pair is more valuable than cycling a fifth card.
