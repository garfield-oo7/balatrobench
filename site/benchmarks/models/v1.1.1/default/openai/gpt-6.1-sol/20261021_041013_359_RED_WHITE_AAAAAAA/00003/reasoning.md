Buy Burglar as an early economy and survival engine. Its three extra hands can yield up to $3 additional unused-hand income each round, repaying the $6 cost quickly. Mad Joker already provides strong early scoring with Two Pair or Full House; if necessary, spare hands can cycle unwanted cards while scoring to find those combinations. Losing Red Deck's discards is a real drawback, but seven hands per round and recurring income justify the investment this early.
