Discard the low off-suit cards while keeping both 9s and K-J-6 of Clubs. This preserves two strong routes: a club flush or improving the pair into a full house/four of a kind. Foil Trading Card adds 50 Chips, making a non-debuffed flush or full house worth roughly 500 points, enough to finish with a simple follow-up hand. Use discards to improve before spending our four hands; Hearts can complete rank combinations but will not contribute card chips against The Head.
