Keep the four hearts and the Steel 4♦, and discard both debuffed spades plus 10♣ to seek a fifth heart. This preserves all four hands while pursuing our level-3 Flush. Ancient Joker now boosts diamonds, not hearts, so a heart flush is a solid scoring step rather than a guaranteed one-hand win. Keep The Chariot available to turn a suitable non-spade replacement into a second held Steel card; using it on a spade would provide no benefit against The Goad.
