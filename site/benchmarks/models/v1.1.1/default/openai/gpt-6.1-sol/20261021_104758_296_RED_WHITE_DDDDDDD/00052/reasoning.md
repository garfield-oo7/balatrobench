Choose The Wheel of Fortune. Five of our six useful Jokers have no edition, so a successful roll gives a permanent scoring upgrade without replacing a Joker or diluting the deck. Death has no enhanced card to copy in this hand, and concentrating further into clubs is not especially valuable because Ancient Joker changes its target suit each round. The Sun and Tower offer little synergy, while Judgement cannot be used with all Joker slots occupied. The Wheel can be used immediately despite our full consumable slots.
