Keep all four Spades and the Steel club Ace; discard the club King, club 8, and heart 2 to find a fifth Spade. A level-6 Spade flush activates Arrowhead and Wrathful on all five scoring cards, with Fibonacci on the 8 and 2. Retaining the Steel Ace adds ×1.5, and removing the heart sets up Blackboard's ×3 provided the new held cards are black. Two discards and five hands remain, so completing our primary flush is preferable to spending a hand on weak pairs.
