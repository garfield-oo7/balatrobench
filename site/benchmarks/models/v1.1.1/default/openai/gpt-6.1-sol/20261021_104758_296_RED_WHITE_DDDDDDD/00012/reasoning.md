Keep both queens and both jacks and draw three toward a full house or four of a kind. Any full house of these faces scores at least (40 + 50 + 150) × 4 = 960, clearing the 600-chip boss in one hand; a scoring spade boosts it further. With three discards available, this is stronger than pursuing a heart flush that usually falls short of 600. If necessary, the retained two pair already scores 360 as a fallback.
