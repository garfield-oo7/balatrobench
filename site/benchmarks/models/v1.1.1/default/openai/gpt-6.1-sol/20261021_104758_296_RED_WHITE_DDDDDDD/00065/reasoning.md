With no discards left, play the three off-suit expendable cards (7♣, 6♦, 3♣) to draw three replacements while preserving all four hearts and the Steel 4♦. This scores a small High Card hand but, more importantly, gives three chances to find the fifth heart. A level-3 heart flush with five Ancient Joker triggers, Even Steven on 8♥ and 2♥, and held Steel should clear the remaining target comfortably. Do not play 3♥ alongside 3♣: the pair's modest points are not worth breaking our four-heart core.
