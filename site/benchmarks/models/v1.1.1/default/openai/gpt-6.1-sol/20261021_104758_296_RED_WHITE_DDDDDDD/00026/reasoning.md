Keep all four spades (10♠, 9♠, 5♠, 2♠) and play the four non-spades to draw four replacements without discards. J♥ scores as High Card, benefiting from Scary Face and Ancient Joker for about 67 chips, and gains Hiker's permanent +5 chips. Any fifth spade completes a flush worth at least (35 + 10 + 9 + 5 + 2 + 2) × (4 + 15 + 10) = 1,827, enough to beat the remaining target. Breaking the four-spade draw for a small pair score would reduce our chance of finishing safely with the two subsequent hands.
