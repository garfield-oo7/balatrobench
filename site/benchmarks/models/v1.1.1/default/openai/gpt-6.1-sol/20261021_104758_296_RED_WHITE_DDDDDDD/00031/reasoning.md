Keep the four diamonds (10♦, 8♦, 3♦, 2♦) and draw four cards toward a flush. The three even diamonds already supply +12 Mult from Even Steven, plus Droll's +10 for a flush, giving at least 26 Mult. A diamond face card adds Scary Face's +30 Chips, while another even diamond adds +4 Mult; either can produce a one-hand clear depending on rank and existing Hiker bonuses. Four discards give us ample opportunity to complete or improve this flush without spending hands. Chasing Ancient's clubs from only two cards is less consistent.
