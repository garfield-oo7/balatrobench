Keep 10♦, 8♦, 3♦, and 2♦ and discard the four non-diamonds to complete our four-card flush draw. Those three even diamonds provide +12 Mult, and Droll adds another +10 on a flush, for at least 26 Mult. A diamond face card also activates Scary Face and should clear 2,000 in one hand; other diamonds provide a strong first hand with all four hands still available. With three discards remaining, preserving the four-diamond foundation is more consistent than restarting from three clubs.
