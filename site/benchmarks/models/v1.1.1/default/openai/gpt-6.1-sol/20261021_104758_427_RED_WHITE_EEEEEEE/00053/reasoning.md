Discard Aces and Queens as Two Pair, including the unneeded King, to activate Burnt Joker and permanently raise our main hand to level 4 (80 Chips, 5 Mult). These odd/face-ranked pairs do not trigger Even Steven, so replacing them is preferable to scoring them against The Flint. Keep the Lucky Eight for an even-ranked pair and draw five fresh cards, with all four hands and three further discards available. The upgrade increases our base scoring despite the boss halving base Chips and Mult.
