Discard Sevens and Fives as Two Pair to activate Burnt Joker, permanently upgrading our main hand from level 4 (80 chips ×5) to level 5 (100 chips ×6). These odd pairs receive no Even Steven bonus, so converting them into a permanent upgrade is better than scoring them now. Retain Eight and Four to seek even-ranked pairs, with all four hands and three further discards available to reach 7,500.
