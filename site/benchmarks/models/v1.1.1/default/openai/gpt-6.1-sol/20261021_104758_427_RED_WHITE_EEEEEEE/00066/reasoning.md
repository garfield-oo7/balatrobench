Play Eights and Twos as level-4 Two Pair, including the Lucky Eight. This scores about 4,500 chips before any Lucky trigger: 100 chips × (5 base +16 Even Steven +8 Jolly +7 Supernova) ×1.25 Hologram. A Lucky Mult trigger wins immediately; otherwise only 500 chips remain with three hands available. Add the unscored Ace to cycle it while retaining the third Eight, Ten, and Six for subsequent pairs. Do not play all three Eights with both Twos: the resulting level-1 Full House scores substantially less.
