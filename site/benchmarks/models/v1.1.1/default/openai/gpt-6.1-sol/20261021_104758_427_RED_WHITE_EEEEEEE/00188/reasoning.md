Apply Justice to the unenhanced Six of Diamonds. Sixes are well represented in this hand and make useful pairs for our level-21 Two Pair build. A scored Glass card doubles the hand's accumulated Mult before Supernova and Mystic Summit activate, providing a larger immediate boost than two Mult enhancements; Blueprint and Hologram then amplify that gain. Avoid overwriting any existing enhancement, and play this Glass Six after additive enhanced scoring cards when possible. Its break risk is acceptable as an optional scoring boost for difficult blinds.
